# Changelog

All notable changes to the UESL protocol are documented here.
Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR`: a MAJOR change alters how UESL is measured or computed (results may not be comparable across major versions); a MINOR change clarifies wording or adds optional guidance.

## [1.0] – 2026-10-01
- Initial public release, presented at the IROS 2026 Workshop "State of the Art in Robotic Leg Prostheses."
- Protocol checklist (Appendix I of the manuscript in preparation), reference calculations (`uesl.py`), benchmark data for 14 walking conditions, and a reporting template.
